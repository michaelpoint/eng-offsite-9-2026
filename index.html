How the block runs

The official agenda blocks 12:30 to 8:00 as a single line — lunch, group activity, dinner. This is how that actually splits.

    Late September on this coast

    71°F / 22°CTypical high 52°F / 11°CTypical low

    This is actually the warm season here, warmer than mid-summer. Mornings often start under a marine layer that burns off by noon, and afternoons are usually clear and mild. The ocean sits around 59°F / 15°C, which is colder than it looks. Bring a layer for the evening. It drops fast once the sun goes.

    Who pays: Point covers lunch, and dinner with drinks. Everything the afternoon itself costs is on you — bike rental, about $7 a head for discs, a few dollars for parking, and, if you spend it in a beer garden, however many beers "one beer" turns out to be. Sort that out with whoever is leading your group. The hotel hang and the redwood hike cost nothing at all, and Capitola only costs whatever coffee you talk yourself into.

    Group leads, book early: every card has a "food recommendations" button with a few lunch and dinner options near that activity. Pick one for your group and call them. Sign-ups close 5 days before the offsite, which is later than most of these places want a headcount, so book on your best estimate in early September and adjust after. Zelda's and The Sand Bar want a reservation well ahead for a table this size, and Showtime takes no reservations at all, so that one is a phone order.

    The whole week

    Tuesday is a travel day — get in when you get in. Drinks and hanging out from 6 isn't something you can be late for; you'll find other Pointers at the hotel bar or wandering around. Any further details will get updated here.

    Getting here: flights are booked. Most people are flying into San Jose (SJC), about 45 minutes from the hotel, and some are coming through SFO, which is more like an hour and a half. Aptos is small and rideshare out here is patchy, so getting from the airport is something we're organising rather than leaving to chance. More information on rideshares coming soon.

    Expenses: Point covers flights, the hotel, and every meal on the agenda — lunches and dinners with drinks. What the afternoon activity itself costs is on you, as is anything personal on top. Keep track of any receipts you need for expensing.
